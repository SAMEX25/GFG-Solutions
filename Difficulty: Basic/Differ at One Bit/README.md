<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/check-whether-two-numbers-differ-at-one-bit-position-only3415/1">Differ at One Bit</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 20px;">Given two non-negative integers <strong>a</strong> and <strong>b</strong>, find if their binary representations differ at exactly one bit position. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>a = 13, b = 9
<strong>Output: </strong>true
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The binary representations of 13 (1101) and 9 (1001) differ at exactly one bit position.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input</strong>: a = 15, b = 8
<strong>Output:</strong> false
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The binary representations of 15 (1111) and 8 (1000) differ at more than one bit position.</span></pre>
